Two Kinds of Cases We Know Inside and Out

Work-Related Car Accidents — Our Specialty

If you were hurt in a crash while driving for work, your case is more complicated than a normal accident claim — and more valuable if it's handled right. You may be owed workers' compensation benefits from your employer's insurer and full injury damages from the at-fault driver.

The two claims affect each other, and insurers on both sides will try to pay less because "the other claim" exists. We run both claims together, in the right order, so each one is maximized. Your consultation is free — and we can usually tell you in one conversation whether you have one claim or two.

Questions People Ask Us

Personal Injury FAQs

How much does it cost to hire a personal injury lawyer in Alpharetta?

Nothing upfront. Your consultation is free, and you pay no fee unless we win your case — we only get paid when you recover compensation.

What if the accident was partly my fault?

You can still recover compensation in Georgia as long as you were less than 50% at fault. Your compensation is reduced by your share of the fault — which is exactly why insurers try to pin more of the blame on you. Don't accept their version of fault before talking to a lawyer.

Should I talk to the insurance company before calling a lawyer?

Talk to us first — it's free. The insurance adjuster's job is to settle your claim for as little as possible, and recorded statements made early are often used to reduce what you're paid. Let us handle those conversations for you.

How long do I have to file a personal injury claim in Georgia?

Generally two years from the date of injury, but deadlines can be shorter in certain cases — so it's best to speak with a lawyer as soon as possible after an accident.

I was hurt in a crash while driving for work. Is that workers' comp or a car accident case?

Often it's both — a workers' compensation claim through your employer's insurance and an injury claim against the at-fault driver. Handling the two together, in the right order, is what we specialize in. Call us and we'll map it out for free.
